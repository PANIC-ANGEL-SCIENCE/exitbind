# Image brief — project-local template

Keep filled personal references and identifiers outside the distributed pack. Remove irrelevant fields; short tasks need only consequential decisions.

- Deliverable: image asset / image-only mockup / working UI integration / prompt-only specification.
- Purpose, audience, context and intended feeling:
- Medium: approved photo / new photo / generated scene or illustration / 3D / diagram; reason:
- Factual status: real product evidence / illustrative concept; details that must not change:
- Target dimensions/format/profile and required narrow/wide crops:
- Subject/action/setting; protected face, hands, garment/product details:
- Camera height, angle, perspective, distance and depth of field:
- Key/fill/environment light; shadow/reflection relationships:
- Material, texture scale, color continuity and authenticity:
- Subject anchors, crop-safe extent and overlay/quiet zones:
- Positive constraints/exclusions; UI stays separate unless image-only deliverable:
- Available capability and authorized production route:

## Reference and permitted-use ledger

| Project-local reference | Actually accessible/inspected? Evidence/date | Observable quality to transfer | Viewing authority | Provider use/upload authority and destination | Public reuse/attribution/license status | Excluded transfer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Real available material only | Seen / unavailable / partial; never infer from URL | Framing/light/material, not guessed traits | Existing authorization or unknown | Existing authorization, not required, or unknown | Exact asset rights or unknown | Unrequested identity or factual-product substitution |

If no reference is available, say so. Label independent samples generic/original, not reference-based. Unknown rights are not permission; independent planning can continue without unavailable material.

## Production instruction

Compose: deliverable/purpose → subject/action/setting → camera/framing → lighting/material → crop/overlay/protected details → palette/finish → exclusions → export. Include supported facts only. Attach approved references through a supported mechanism when authorized; mentioning one does not establish visual conditioning.
