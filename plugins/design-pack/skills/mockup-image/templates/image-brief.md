# Image work note — project-local template

Use only consequential fields. Face, expression and casting fields apply only when that evidence is visible or required; intentional outfit-only and object-only modes do not need invented people. Keep private sources and filled evidence outside this package.

- Target frame, receiving layout/crops and intended display size:
- Visual subject / framing mode: portrait, worn outfit, wearer-view detail, object or another task-specific mode; required visible evidence and intentional exclusions:
- Source-frame evidence: full original boundary inspected versus thumbnail/viewport crop; mirror, wearer view or uncertain capture mode:
- Fictional identity supplied by the brief versus chosen presentation; individual motives/context and concrete visible decisions, no inferred reference identity:
- Cross-group pose/expression evidence: inspected differences, within-group variation, overlap, counterexamples and unknown attributes:
- New pose/view intention: front/profile/three-quarter/back/partial or obscured view as relevant; support/action, body-camera relationship, required visibility, meaningful change and protected constants:
- If multi-person: requested count, each person's role/position/action, shared attention or intentional separation, contact/occlusion ownership, actual people versus reflections; fictional relationship versus unknown real ties:
- Captured moment; observed gaze/expression/body/action/camera relationships versus inferred feeling:
- Subject/viewpoint, light, material and evidence to preserve; identity distinct from behavior:
- Representative reference coverage, inspected detail and observation → transferable decision → output check:
- Casting/presence; pose; gaze/expression; hair; makeup; accessories; garment tension; subject-camera relation:

- Intended stance / participation as a visual hypothesis, not inferred profession; decisive attention/body, styling/wear and camera cues versus competing reading:
- Expression intensity independently of attitude; what carries the moment, what can stay quiet, and unsupported facial cue stacking to remove:
- Subject-continuity diagnosis when needed: exact current image; head/hair scale, facial projection, neck/shoulder connection, posture/perspective/occlusion and capture-response evidence versus uncertainty; no identity inference or fixed ratio:

- Tattoo/piercing/accessory transfer: form/scale/location, skin or fastening relationship, pose/reflection/occlusion, material/light and required visible detail; invented motives distinct from observed source:

- Makeup when requested: observed eye/brow/cheek/lip/skin placement and finish versus lighting/unknown detail; chosen character-specific emphasis or absence, palette relation and actual visibility, no gender-exclusive default:
- Continuity repair boundary: connected region to adjust, accepted action/view/outfit/marks/space/light to preserve, any necessary coupled change and actual visibility limits:
- Correction scope: failed axis → successful task cues to preserve → actual-pixel recheck; protected sources and unrelated changes excluded:
- Capture texture separately: perspective, light, tonal response, detail/compression; requested device class versus unverified provenance:
- Framing/moment: camera height, distance and angle; subject scale/placement, receiving crop, obstructions and background involvement; recurring relationships and counterexamples:
- If mirror selfie: actual capture mode, camera/mirror/person arrangement, phone height/angle and face occlusion, hand/arm chain, reflection asymmetries and room/light continuity; observed frame coordinates versus uncertain handedness:
- Lighting evidence by source role: lit/shaded planes, source hypotheses, subject/background brightness, falloff and local color; scene transfer, unknown provenance and studio-light drift:
- Clothing as worn: relative volumes/lengths, layering/openings, closure, sleeve/hem behavior, material/light and accessory placement; visible evidence, fixed garments and authorized changes:
- Defining fashion relationships: actual silhouette/cut, panel/closure boundaries, layer lengths, material contrast and wear; retained choices, permitted departures and where each stays visible:
- Local repair risk: defect to fix versus source-specific relationships to preserve; generic substitution or hidden garment evidence to reject:
- Material/finish correction: observed surface, thickness, bending, tension and contact defects; support-to-finish path, visible versus cropped reference evidence, authorized illustrative additions:
- Repair protection: latest accepted subject/outfit/camera/light regions, clean current source and transition areas to compare; no unrelated recast or restyle:
- Candidate differences on multiple direction axes, held constants and near-duplicate rejection:
- Color/treatment scope: photograph versus UI/presentation or other medium:
- Intended scene reading: styling/use, activity, social/spatial relation and camera; observed versus inferred:
- Competing reading and scene/crop study before committing a master:
- Authenticity/context requirements, separate from photographic craft:
- Exact copy and selected state if image-only; live text/UI source if implemented:
