# Mockup image

Make imagery that belongs in its intended composition. Apply within [Design Pack](../design-pack/SKILL.md). A prompt is not an image, a static mockup is not working UI, and photorealism is not factual evidence.

For an explicit whole-scene reset, use the repair/reset method in [image production](references/image-production.md) before preserving old masters. Couple the reopened subject, styling and space with the camera, and inspect actual organized references through [source roles](../design-pack/references/reference-roles.md).

When a visible designed object needs an explainable, showable identity, use the supporting-artifact method in [image production](references/image-production.md). Make and inspect its master first, then verify only what the scene actually depicts and makes legible.

For molded-looking garments or weak finishing, use [capture and styling](references/capture-styling.md) to inspect material behavior and support-to-hem continuity. Preserve the user's accepted regions during a narrow correction; added texture or trim is not proof of success.

Choose the requested visual subject before directing a face. Use [capture and styling](references/capture-styling.md) for portrait, outfit-led, wearer-view and object-only framing; do not turn intentionally faceless or item-led references into compulsory model portraits.

For varied people or poses, use [model direction](references/model-direction.md) for individual self-presentation and [capture and styling](references/capture-styling.md) for purposeful front, side, back or obscured views. Identity labels do not prescribe a pose. Use [image production](references/image-production.md) when fictional tattoos or accessories need concrete, consistent reasons and designs rather than decorative biography.

For a disconnected face/body impression, use [model direction](references/model-direction.md) to inspect head-neck-shoulder-body continuity and [image production](references/image-production.md) for a bounded repair. Keep this separate from gender identity, and preserve successful pose, wardrobe and deliberate visibility rather than replacing the whole character by default.

For style-context or outfit-intent feedback, read the coupled material/silhouette/layer method in [capture and styling](references/capture-styling.md) before choosing colors. Use [image production](references/image-production.md) when inherited tattoos, accessories or fictional motives no longer fit the user's current direction. Matching a palette or naming a mood does not establish coherence.

For accumulated feedback, first reconcile the current [brief](../design-pack/references/briefing.md) and applicable private profile. Carry active changes and preservation requirements into this image; supported modes are not a checklist of everything to include. Keep project-specific item semantics and writing-style sources separate from reusable image methods, and record actual consumption rather than assuming it from a version label.

For generic garment drift after a color or category change, resolve the construction brief in [capture and styling](references/capture-styling.md) before generating: inspect the outline and volume, connect them to visible cut/support/material, then compare the output against the source and rejected fallback. Distinctive does not mean more arbitrary decoration.

For saved fashion or model preferences, load the applicable actual private guidance through [profile memory](../design-pack/references/memory.md) before production. Current task overrides project direction, which overrides the relevant user default. Record missing or denied reads honestly; this method is not automatic host loading.

For background removal or transparent subject assets, use [background removal](references/background-removal.md). Preserve product detail, separate RGB from alpha changes and review difficult edges and translucent material on multiple backgrounds; generation is not a promise of exact extraction.

## Choose and make

For reference-led people/model imagery or casting alternatives, read [model direction](references/model-direction.md) before prompting. Apply its facial checks only to the visible or required person evidence, not every phone-camera or object image.

For photographic framing, available-light relationships or worn outfits, read [capture and styling](references/capture-styling.md). For a style-specific brief, retain the defining cut, material and layer relationships through recasting and local repairs; compare the actual output instead of accepting generic garment labels. Compare actual camera placement, light distribution and garment relationships before choosing a device label or polished product-display default.

For a requested reflected self-portrait, read [mirror selfie](references/mirror-selfie.md) before generation. Review the actual phone/hand, reflection, lighting and requested face/body life-stage relationship rather than merely adding a phone.

1. Identify the deliverable, requested medium, use context, target frame and required fidelity. Preserve an explicitly requested photograph or other medium; missing capability is a limit, not permission to substitute.
2. Personally inspect the actual relevant references and assets. Use [Discovery](../design-pack/references/discovery.md) and [source roles](../design-pack/references/reference-roles.md). Separate content/identity, craft benchmark, edit target and comparison-only inputs. Respect existing viewing, provider-use and redistribution scope.
3. Build the scene from its required subject, captured moment, viewpoint, light and crop; include expression/body relationships when relevant. Use [image production](references/image-production.md). Relate styling, activity, context and camera without equating appearance with membership. Judge age/life-stage, intended attitude and facial expression intensity independently; a repair to one must preserve successful cues on the others. Calibrate expression to the actual moment instead of stacking intense facial cues to prove attitude. Authentic context can include deliberate pose, styling and excellent photography.
4. Follow the [generation contract](references/generation-contract.md) for actual supported image inputs, compact prompts and native output evidence. Produce only when the task includes production; a pack-edit request alone does not authorize live samples.
5. Choose asset-first, composition-first or coupled exploration from the uncertainty. Preserve selected clean masters for fidelity and reuse; a master is not immune to rejection. Keep exact text/UI editable when needed and repair the relevant source layer.
6. Open actual output and receiving crops at whole-view, intended-use and source-detail scales. Compare composition and any required expression with actual benchmark pixels, as well as applicable geometry, visible anatomy and protected detail. Review overworked photographic surfaces separately from expression; preserve an accepted layout and repair only evidenced causes. Reinspect unchanged regions after edits. A technically sound image that misses the central art direction remains in rework.

For hero boards or sequences, load [portfolio presentation](../design-pack/references/portfolio-presentation.md). Matched boards preferably share clean asset and screen sources, while differing in narrative role. An inspected prior board may be a disclosed continuity input when authorized; it does not replace original source detail or make the next board independent.

Only an explicit untouched-output evaluation uses [first pass](../design-pack/references/first-pass.md). Freeze its asset, inspect it, compose the first screen, then freeze the complete result before review. Ordinary work may refine within its existing authority.

Keep optional [image briefs](templates/image-brief.md), filled receipts, source identities and generated trials outside this pack. Retain required asset notices. Structural checks, model settings and prompt detail do not prove photographic quality, source fidelity or owner acceptance.
