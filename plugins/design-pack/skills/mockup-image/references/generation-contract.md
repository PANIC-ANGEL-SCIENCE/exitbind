# Generate without losing the source

A requested reflected self-portrait uses the [mirror selfie](mirror-selfie.md) method before generation. Keep the actual reflected-camera arrangement and requested life-stage consistency explicit; inspect returned phone, hand, reflection and face/body relationships rather than treating the prompt as proof.

Use [capture and styling](capture-styling.md) when camera placement, available light or garment relationships drive the photographic brief. Carry a few observed relationships into the actual call; avoid replacing them with a device label, catalogue pose or a long inventory of objects.

Use when a generator makes or edits an image. This is a production method, not a provider integration. Follow the current host tool and image skill. Keep completed prompts, calls, sources and outputs in the private project.

## Choose the production route

A whole-board generation can be useful for atmosphere, exploratory composition or an explicitly image-only deliverable. It is less reliable for exact copy, repeated identity and many precise states. Choose asset-first for critical source detail, composition-first for uncertain hierarchy, or coupled studies for image/type/crop relationships. Once the direction holds, separate clean assets and editable text/UI where precision or reuse warrants it. This is a risk-based choice, not a universal production sequence. Do not silently change the requested medium or claim editable source when only a raster exists.

For [portfolio presentations](../../design-pack/references/portfolio-presentation.md), separate board frame from each screen's natural viewport. Develop product and presentation together where they affect each other, then resolve readable states before final staging. A static image proposes behavior; only an implemented and tested transition demonstrates it.

State the task-selected subject and framing mode from [capture and styling](capture-styling.md) in the actual production call. Identify required visible evidence and intentional exclusions. Do not carry an old portrait, face requirement or full-body default into a new outfit-only or object-only request. Likewise, do not crop away a currently protected face or object to evade a failed requirement. Map references by their role so an object's printed imagery, a garment flat-lay and a person are not silently substituted for each other.

For a new person or pose, carry the selected self-presentation, action/support, body/camera relationship and intended visibility into the actual call, following [model direction](model-direction.md) and [capture and styling](capture-styling.md). Do not substitute a gender or orientation label for those directions, or silently reuse an old front-facing pose. A request for one new draft does not authorize a multi-view batch. State protected constants and intentional face exclusion without hiding required evidence.

If fictional tattoos or accessories matter, bind any actual design master separately from the principal person's identity and state which features must survive at output scale. Written motives are fictional context, not image evidence. After generation inspect the visible placement, design and pose distinction; do not mark unseen details or an imagined interview as visually verified.

For a requested multi-person scene, state count, individual roles and the shared action/spacing rather than concatenating portrait prompts. Follow [capture and styling](capture-styling.md) for contact, occlusion and any reflections. Inspect actual people count separately from reflected instances, individual distinction and required detail. A source-group label is not proof of friendship or authority to add people.

## Bind actual inputs

Personally inspect selected pixels on the producing host. Give each image its [source role](../../design-pack/references/reference-roles.md): content/identity, visual relationship, edit target, continuity or comparison-only. State what must survive and what may change. Keep only relevant inputs; more references can dilute the intended relationship.

Use the tool's supported image-input parameter, not a path or source name inside the prompt. Verify local files on the host making the call, or map the smallest supported set of conversation images that contains every required input. Never combine mutually exclusive mechanisms or invent an attachment. Maker viewing, provider input and visible transfer are three different facts.

Respect existing task authority for viewing, provider submission and redistribution. If required conditioning is unavailable or unauthorized, report the exact limitation before dependent production. Continue independent work; do not quietly omit a required input or switch providers to hide the gap. Text-only generation is valid when appropriate and permitted, not equivalent to image conditioning. Prior rejected/comparison-only outputs are not hidden edit targets for a new independent concept.

## Remove stale anchors for an authorized reset

Before a whole new direction, apply the reset/repair distinction in [image production](image-production.md). Map the latest reopened axes and still-current constraints, then inspect both submitted image arguments and inherited prompt text for rejected identity, garment, space or composition anchors. A prior output may be inspected for comparison without becoming a provider input. Use original permitted source relationships or a new text-only direction as appropriate; do not disguise another descendant edit as a fresh start.

For sectioned references, carry the selected roles and conflicts from [source roles](../../design-pack/references/reference-roles.md) into one coherent scene, not a face/outfit/room collage. Record what was actually read before generation and retain the actual call/input map. A declared reset or version label does not prove that old conditioning was removed.

For a fashion-led brief, carry the selected source-defining cut, material, layer and wear relationships from [capture and styling](capture-styling.md) into the submitted prompt or approved image conditioning. Verify that a later casting change or local repair has not silently replaced them with generic garment labels. A detailed research note not carried into production is not applied direction. The actual output must be compared independently with the selected references; neither prompt wording nor attached inputs proves faithful transfer.

When outfit intent is the correction, state the coupled material/silhouette/layer/wear relationships from [capture and styling](capture-styling.md) in the actual call, not just the palette or a mood word. Map the current image to protected person/pose/scene roles and the selected style source to its intended wardrobe role; explicitly reopen rejected garments and personal details instead of preserving them as stale conditioning. Use only supported and authorized inputs. A source image's presence does not prove its material or styling transferred.

If the latest feedback reopens a fictional tattoo or accessory, use the actual revised master and current character account through [image production](image-production.md); do not silently carry the old mark or retrospectively assign it a new identity. State unknown source regions and original design decisions without inventing reference evidence. Review outfit-intent fit, personal-detail coherence, required graphic transfer and protected regions independently in the returned image. A palette pass or plausible story cannot override a failed required relationship.

## Bind designed supporting artifacts

When a task requires a concrete designed object, follow the supporting-artifact method in [image production](image-production.md). Keep the full master, selected crop/side and intended visible features distinct from fictional narrative. Map the exact current asset to the actual supported image argument; do not claim a prose description conditioned the generator or reuse an unrelated old fragment as its source.

Verify the returned object's graphic hierarchy, orientation, occlusion and material response at native and use scale. Master legibility, submitted conditioning and legibility in the scene are independent facts. A generated approximation is not exact reproduction. Use a companion view for information too small to show naturally; do not silently replace the required scene depiction with that companion. Repair or disclose an unsupported fidelity requirement rather than inventing a successful transfer.

## Direct relationships, not an illustrated checklist

Match the prompt to the stage. For exploration, state the purpose, actual content, decisive visual relationship, source roles and genuine boundaries. Leave unconstrained space for composition. For a resolved production edit, specify exact copy, supported geometry, protected regions and the narrow change. Do not turn exploratory judgment into a rectangle-by-rectangle recipe unless that geometry is the requirement.

Describe why elements relate: a crop reveals an object's tension, a gaze and camera angle establish distance, a selection grows from its subject, or type changes the balance of an image. Use concrete visible decisions rather than a list of prestige adjectives. A face category and wardrobe do not convey expression or participation. Connect observed styling/use, activity, social or spatial relation and camera/gaze/body when relevant, without claiming authenticity from appearance.

Scope color and treatment explicitly to photography, interface or presentation. Exact required copy and truthful state changes remain constraints, but compliance does not determine the entire visual solution. Keep exclusions few and concrete. More constraints cannot compensate for a weak concept; reopen the study instead of appending every prior criticism. No task-specific palette, pose or source motif becomes a pack default.

For people/model candidates, use the separate direction and capture axes in [model direction](model-direction.md). Carry decisive observed relationships into each prompt; a device label or generic aesthetic adjective cannot replace them. Plan useful differences before generation and inspect the returned pixels before counting outputs as distinct alternatives. For corrections, state the failed axis and the successful cues to preserve. Separate expression intensity from attitude before prompting: describe attention/action and only necessary facial cues, not an amplified stare/smirk/pout bundle. A request to reduce expression must preserve successful styling, pose, casting and camera relationships. If the photograph also feels overworked, inspect and repair evidenced surface defects separately; do not hide them with blur/grain or redesign an accepted layout. Keep age/life-stage, intended stance, expression fit/intensity, styling/wear, camera relationship and capture texture separately reviewable; a job title, harder stare or compliant prompt cannot establish attitude.

For a garment material or finishing repair, state the accepted source and protected regions, the visible defect, and which relevant construction/finish references are actually provided. Distinguish observed source detail from an authorized illustrative design choice where the source is cropped. Follow [capture and styling](capture-styling.md) for material response and whole-garment continuity. After the supported edit, inspect the repaired region, its transition and the full image; a better surface or added fastening does not prove preserved identity or coherent construction.

For a subject-continuity repair, identify the exact current edit source, observed head/face/neck/shoulder-body mismatch, bounded connected region and protected scene requirements. Use [model direction](model-direction.md) to state the relationship to change instead of a gendered feature list. Preserve intentional pose and face visibility; disclose any necessary coupled adjustment. After the supported edit, compare transition and whole figure as well as protected clothes, marks, light and framing. The correct identity label, a prettier face or a plausible limb chain cannot establish that the subject reads coherently.

When later instructions reopen build, tattoo/accessory design or outfit colors, update the change/preserve boundary instead of carrying a stale absolute lock. Carry the actual chosen body action, attached-detail placement and palette relationships through the call, using [capture and styling](capture-styling.md). State source roles and any required design master without treating private reference appearance as demographic truth. Review tattoo/attachment and palette fidelity independently from face/body coherence.

## Preserve masters and revise locally

Save each native original before editing. Where precision or continuity matters, keep clean photo/object masters, editable UI/type and scene/frame layers independently reusable. Recompose a new board from these sources rather than extracting its photo from a previous flattened board. A first board may be a disclosed continuity input, but does not become the sole master of every element.

Choose the repair layer from the defect. Copy changes belong in type, crop changes in placement, state changes in UI, and subject changes in the image source. A failed organizing idea may require replacement rather than a narrow edit. If a whole-image edit is appropriate or the only supported route, state the change and preserve the original. Compare protected regions with clean sources: texture, edges, anatomy, joins, glyphs and tonal detail. Do not pass a softened edit back as the next master. Return to a clean source or disclose the unresolved limit. Upscaling/sharpening is not recovered source detail.

This does not mandate manual compositing or prohibit generative edits. Use the method suited to the deliverable and available capability; be honest about exactness. A no-repair trial follows [first pass](../../design-pack/references/first-pass.md).

## Verify the returned artifact

Record intended design viewport, raster request and frame kind separately: asset, one viewport, scrollable page or presentation. Only supported output parameters are enforced; prose requests are not settings. Measure actual native dimensions. An equal aspect ratio can represent a proportional viewport; a long page cannot establish that its contents fit one phone screen.

Do not stretch, crop away required evidence or pad an output and call it compliant native generation. Preserve the original and identify every derivative or revision. A requested exact export can have a documented transform; scaling alone creates no new detail.

Open the result at whole-image, intended-use and source-detail scales beside relevant sources. Inspect actual language/copy, crop and hierarchy, source associations, anatomy/geometry, material/light and unchanged regions. For a sequence, verify **action → visible change → next action → resulting state**, with the required initial state and retained selection/back context. The number and arrangement of states follow the task. Labels such as “step two” are not evidence that the depicted content changed.

Repair and reopen in ordinary work; preserve and disclose defects in a frozen trial. Keep visual craft, factual fidelity, accessibility, functionality and owner acceptance separate. Correct states or clean geometry cannot close an unmet central art-direction goal. One successful output cannot establish a causal pack improvement or general reliability.

## Keep a useful receipt, not a substitute for review

Keep the actual prompt/call, image input map, native output/dimensions, revision parent and changed/preserved source identities. Record only settings returned by the host. A supported file or display result is needed before claiming an output exists; verify transferred bytes on a receiving host.

The optional [image run](../templates/image-run.json) and checker validate declared prompt/input bindings, hashes, supported dimension headers and review order. Ordinary receipts do not require a freeze event; the legacy first-output marker is optional. Only explicit frozen trials require immediate freezing before review and forbid further generation in that trial. They cannot authenticate execution, prove perception, measure aesthetics or test live behavior. Records with honest unresolved defects are valid evidence. Do not spend more effort completing receipts than resolving the artifact.
