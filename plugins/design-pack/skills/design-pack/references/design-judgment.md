# Design judgment

Decide what the audience should notice, feel or understand, then make that intention visible. Calm, intense, sparse and dense are available; none establishes quality.

## Diagnose before preserving

View the current work beside the requested benchmarks. Separate concept, assets, composition and finish. A correct flow may have weak art direction; flawless anatomy may carry the wrong expression. Identify the visible relationship that misses the brief, not just defects that are easy to count.

Recheck preservation claims after a rejection. User-fixed requirements remain; a designer-selected name, metaphor, layout or asset can be replaced within authorized rework. Do not let prior effort make it untouchable. An explicit frozen trial remains read-only.

Scope constraints by medium through [source roles](reference-roles.md). A control palette does not automatically grade photography. Subject identity does not specify expression. Judge subject, styling, activity and camera together; visible cues do not prove cultural membership. Transfer relationships, not stereotypes.

## Build the uncertain relationship

Choose the cheapest useful construction with actual content: crop/type study, spatial thumbnail, state strip, scene or animatic. Test an alternative organizing relationship when that is uncertain; do not require a variant count or alternatives after the direction is clear.

Choose production order by risk. Asset-first suits critical subject detail or repeated identity. Composition-first suits an uncertain overall silhouette. Coupled studies suit image/type/crop relationships neither can settle alone. Separate reusable sources once useful; do not polish a master before learning that it cannot carry the composition.

Choose on the rendered result: first-read hierarchy, image/type scale, content association, rhythm, expression and intended-use fit. State the consequential tradeoff briefly. An articulate explanation cannot rescue an inert study.

## Resolve the work

- **Type:** use actual words/scripts. Compare dark mass, line silhouette, counters, baseline and role contrast.
- **Image:** inspect gaze, expression, pose, silhouette, light and detail in the receiving crop. A technically clean but miscast image needs a new direction, not sharpening.
- **Space:** relate visible forms and semantic groups. Empty space and continuous intensity each need composition.
- **State:** make the useful transformation visible, with object identity, readable action and recovery.
- **Finish:** resolve tangencies, optical edges, material joins and exports without flattening the chosen expression.

## Decide whether the outcome is met

Review the product without staging, then review the integrated presentation. These answer different questions; neither excuses failure in the other. Compare to benchmark pixels at equivalent scales, not only to the weaker previous version.

State craft, functional/accessibility and delivery findings separately. A craft failure central to the brief remains blocking even if the file is deliverable and the action chain is correct. If only local finish fails, repair that layer. If the visual proposition still fails, discard or rebuild it within scope. Do not rename an unresolved core weakness a minor limitation to end the cycle.

No score or review ritual applies. Name the failed relationship and next construction. Another persona is not independent evidence. Technical checks, reviewer judgment and actual user acceptance are distinct; never attribute acceptance not received.
