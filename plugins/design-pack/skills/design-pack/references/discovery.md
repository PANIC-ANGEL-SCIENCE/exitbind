# Discover the material needed for this decision

Research serves a concrete design question. Use available project assets and approved identity first. New directions may need a close task/state analogue, an expressive contrast or a useful type/material specimen. Select sources for those questions rather than touring a fixed gallery. This pack has no source catalog and needs no private calibration access to operate.

## Inspect before borrowing

Resolve the brief's primary action, medium, actual language, target scale and intended feeling. Infer low-risk details within delegated authority. A short request does not imply a particular palette, system font, sparsity or evaluation mode.

Open the relevant actual image, screen, specimen or video through authorized tools. A page title, prompt, README, search thumbnail or another agent's description is navigation, not your pixel inspection. Distinguish an actual product screen from a promotional cover, angled device or material staging. Inspect the part that answers the design question at useful size.

Keep a private [reference session](../templates/reference-session.md): exact source/version, observer/time, state or frame, viewed scale/capture, visible observations and the relationship to transfer. Distinguish measurement, estimate and inference. Source appearance does not establish usability, commercial success, current runtime behavior or redistribution rights.

For UI load [interface craft](interface-craft.md), and for type load [typography](typography.md), before their consequential decisions. Convert what was seen into a proposal: relationship → actual-content composition/type/image/state decision → visible failure condition. Preserve contextual choices rather than copying coordinates or an entire identity.

## When access fails

Retain the exact attempted material and tool error. Try a bounded relevant alternative using the same medium, action and state, preferably a maker's primary demonstration or official support material. A reachable desktop cover cannot establish a missing mobile selection state. A style contrast may inform expression while leaving that state unknown.

After a small targeted search, continue independent planning and mark unsupported source-derived direction provisional. If the missing pixels are essential to placing content or completing the same task, keep dependent composition unfinished and identify that exact input. Do not silently replace an unavailable supplied asset with a guessed image. Do not change security/network policy or buy access as an implicit research step.

## Transfer and handoff

Give an authorized maker exact materials and state/scale notes; confirm readable local files or a supported image attachment on the receiving host, then they inspect the pixels before composition. A path on the sender's host is not a transfer. For UI follow the [screen production](screen-production.md) input contract. Your observations do not become their firsthand evidence. Use [reference analysis](reference-analysis.md) to distinguish task fit, expressive fit and misleading presentation effects. In ordinary production, inspect the created artifact and refine; [first pass](first-pass.md) applies only to an explicitly selected trial.

Work needs actual title, paragraph, label and mixed-numeral specimens in its required languages and scripts. Inspect real available family/weight files, optical size, line breaks, fallback and rights. A familiar family name or availability alone cannot choose the voice.

Private sources, owner calibration and asset permissions stay in the authorized project. The blank [private overlay](../templates/private-reference-overlay.json) may record existing project evidence; populating it is optional and does not create a new shared registry. Viewing, provider submission and public redistribution are distinct uses. Retain required asset notices where redistribution is authorized.
