# Study the mechanism, then build it

Use for a new or rejected direction. Start with a concrete design question; a local correction needs no research tour. Use [Discovery](discovery.md) for source access and [source roles](reference-roles.md) before transferring aesthetics.

## Inspect comparable evidence

View the exact requested images, frames or product states at useful size. A title, gallery cover or another worker's summary cannot establish the unseen work. Select a close task analogue and useful contrasts when they answer the question; no source count is a target. Keep current versus historical captures, observed pixels versus author claims, and missing coverage distinct.

Compare these relationships rather than collecting adjectives:

- Image, type and open-space masses at whole-frame scale.
- Scale changes, crop anchors, depth, edges and optical alignments.
- Display, reading, control and annotation type with actual language.
- Lightness, chroma and material relationships across occupied areas and separately scoped media.
- Gaze, expression, body support/action and camera relationship when people carry the intended effect.
- Information/state changes inside UI versus staging outside it.
- Rhythm across a sequence, including what each new view contributes.

A polished screenshot does not establish conversion, usability or the maker's process. State process reconstructions as hypotheses. Record source identifiers, observations and rights in the private project.

## Convert analysis into construction

For each useful mechanism, write one compact row: **source/state → visible relationship → likely effect → new construction → comparison view**. Then make the construction with this project's content. For example, a reference's close-detail/whole-object contrast could become a crop study and a paired evidence view; its exact object, palette and coordinates need not transfer. Note the causal relationship, not just its visible ingredients: a head angle without the associated gaze, camera height or body action may produce a different effect. Treat the inferred effect as a hypothesis until the new artifact supports it.

Use [visual methods](visual-methods.md) for actual-content studies. Do not count a renamed mood, long prompt, source list or filled brief as a design alternative. Compare the output with the source relationship at equivalent whole-frame and readable-detail scales. Record what visibly transferred and what did not.

When feedback rejects the direction, identify the unsupported assumption: content role, expression, photo quality, type voice, state behavior, spatial structure or presentation rhythm. Recheck which requirements were truly fixed and which choices were the maker's. Do not turn criticism into a universal blacklist or a replacement default such as dark-and-bold. Reopen or replace the construction that caused the gap; refining its geometry does not preserve its legitimacy.

## Combine sources without averaging

Separate repeated cross-source relationships, local motifs and current task fit. Repetition supports a preference hypothesis, not a permanent instruction. Current explicit direction overrides inferred taste. Choose a compatible intersection and keep useful tensions; do not collage every source or strip each down to its safest familiar control.

For [portfolio presentation](portfolio-presentation.md), inspect actual section order. Analyze presentation craft separately from the embedded product. A new subject reference does not replace an earlier explicit quality benchmark. Keep source names and completed synthesis outside the distributable.

## Make copy specific

Each line should identify, orient, enable action, explain state, supply supported detail or carry intentional narrative/voice. Remove filler that only makes empty space look designed. Do not invent metrics, counts, endorsements, provenance or product properties. Mark illustrative samples honestly.

Try deletion and substitution: if removing a line changes neither meaning nor intended atmosphere, it may be unnecessary; if it fits any unrelated product unchanged, add substance or omit it. These are judgment aids, not bans on short labels or expressive writing.

Deliver the artifact, important source coverage limits and a few consequential choices. Reference counts, written analysis and structural tests do not establish a better-looking result.
