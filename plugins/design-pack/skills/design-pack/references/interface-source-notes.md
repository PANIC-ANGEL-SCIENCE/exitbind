## Useful delta from 0.3.0

The previous pack already covered optical alignment, motion, typography, color and states. [Interface craft](interface-craft.md) adds a concrete separation between reference fidelity, exploration, state workbench, difficult-input fixtures and review; actual component/style reuse; reachable branches and realistic data at established seams; evidence that fixtures actually arrived; and state/viewport/evidence/cause-specific findings. Image overlays are checked over the actual crop and alpha composition, not a neutral swatch. These methods are independently phrased and integrated with the pack's existing authority and first-pass boundaries.

Nested radii, optical icon balance and repeated-action motion are contextual refinements. Exact upstream press scale, blur, stagger, icon stroke mapping, pure image outline, one-accent/hue rule, type weight threshold, Latin measure/tracking and font smoothing are not universal defaults. Screenshot scale remains estimated unless known. Do not reproduce inaccessible behavior just for fidelity, suppress zoom through transforms, or import mandatory provider, subagent, no-debug or one-look policies. Expose the conflict and follow the authorized task and accessibility requirements.

A separate coordinator-supplied research review confirmed the MIT notice and the same nested-corner, optical-icon, repeated-motion and state-check themes. This is corroborating source interpretation, not rendered verification. Fixed press/stagger examples remain optional author examples.
