# Turn a reference into a built relationship

Use after [reference analysis](reference-analysis.md). The useful unit is **visible source relationship → construction move → artifact comparison**. A filled decision card cannot replace the construction move.

## Make a small executable proposal

Keep the proposition short enough to act on: actual subject, intended reading/feeling, organizing relationship, permitted sources and the visible condition that would make it fail. Put exact values in the project after testing, not in this reusable method.

Make a study in the medium that exposes the uncertainty:

| Uncertainty | Useful study | Compare |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Composition | Real-content thumbnails with image/type masses | First read, scale contrast, edge tension, open/occupied rhythm |
| Typography | Identical actual copy at intended role sizes | Script mass, line endings, spacing and voice |
| Imagery | Clean source crops placed in the layout | Evidence retained, silhouette, context, local reading backgrounds |
| Interaction | Adjacent states or a small usable transition | What changes, identity, action clarity, interruption/return |
| Material | Simple scene or physical swatch under intended light | Contact, scale, reflection, joins and factual limits |
| Sequence | Panel strip or low-cost animatic | Role of each view, visual transition, repetition versus development |

Study count and fidelity follow uncertainty. Ordinary exploratory work may render alternatives; explicit first-output trials plan without competing rendered outputs under [first pass](first-pass.md). Compare like with like: superior photography in only one option biases a layout comparison.

## Build in dependencies

Choose asset-first, composition-first or coupled exploration according to the uncertain relationship. Before final placement, inspect actual source assets and bind annotations to their real crops; use real language before locking space and natural screen proportions before final device perspective. Geometry, light, texture and timing may need coupled studies. These dependencies protect a resolved design, not a mandatory order for every exploratory decision.

Keep reusable parts independent: image masters, text, screen source, frame/scene and annotations. A flattened preview is a review artifact, not a clean master for every next revision. Recompose from sources when possible. If the tool only produces whole-board rasters, preserve originals, disclose fidelity limits and compare unchanged regions before accepting an edit.

## Compose and refine

Choose a grid, content-led alignment, type field or distributed structure because it supports the subject. Relate visible letterforms and image anchors, not only bounding boxes. Stable hit areas can surround optically adjusted marks. Bind image annotations to its displayed crop coordinates.

Inspect thumbnail mass first. At reading size inspect grouping, actual words, selected state and subject association. At detail size inspect edges, tangencies, clipped glyphs, repeated texture, material contact and compression. A decorative layer that passes a thumbnail check can still corrupt fine information.

For effects, specify only consequential parameters: scale, placement, contrast, frequency or time; intended benefit; fallback where needed; and a visible failure condition. Budget runtime, motion or production cost when the environment makes it relevant. Texture, gradients, shadows and overlap are techniques, not automatic defects or quality guarantees.

## Compare the whole relationship

For a connected deliverable, make and inspect the sequence strip before polishing isolated frames, then inspect the final sequence again after export. For people, compare styling, activity, context and camera as one visible scene; adding a scene-signaling object to the same generic pose is not evidence of a solved relationship. For explicit shape and naming feedback, test the actual controls and strings across affected states, not just swatches or tokens. These checks apply to the current task, not every project.

## Repair the cause

- Priority unclear: redistribute masses or change their contrast before increasing every label.
- Repeated screens tell the same story: change the state or evidence, not just the phone angle.
- Selection feels detached: test its spatial origin and transformed content, with a stable accessible alternative.
- Type lacks character: revise the actual role relationships, not only the family name.
- Photo detail is degrading: return to its clean master; sharpening a damaged board does not recover evidence.
- Texture obscures reading: change scale, adjacency or backing where the failure occurs.
- Direction still feels generic after polish: reopen the organizing idea rather than add more ornaments.

These are diagnoses, not automatic prescriptions. A photo-led concept may be appropriate, identical screens may demonstrate a real comparison, and a familiar pattern may best serve the task. Explain the choice through the brief and observed result.

Use [validation](validation.md) to record artifact/location → observation → consequence → repair → retest. Preserve approved meaning, accessible operation and truthful claims. A frozen trial reports failures read-only. Neither checklist completion nor source resemblance proves artistic success.
