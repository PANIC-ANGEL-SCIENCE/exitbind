# Construct an aesthetic

Use for appearance-led work. Atmosphere, delight, tension, richness and beauty are legitimate purposes; no fabricated business benefit is needed. Use [visual methods](visual-methods.md) to build the proposition and [typography](typography.md) for type decisions.

## Start with material, not adjectives

Put actual title/copy, images or objects into the working frame. Identify their useful properties: a long word, uneven silhouette, bright seam, repeated action, large numeric difference or temporal pause. Choose a relationship that turns those properties into the intended experience. “Premium” is not a construction instruction.

## Couple choices in small studies

Build only the studies that resolve uncertainty. Keep content, fidelity and target size comparable.

- **Mass:** reduce image, type and material regions to simple shapes. Move their scale, crop and overlap until the intended first read or distributed rhythm is evident. A thumbnail that needs a long explanation has not resolved its visual argument.
- **Type:** set the actual title in available faces and weights. Compare line silhouettes, then tune the selected voice with readable text and labels. Do not evaluate a display face on an alphabet alone.
- **Image:** compare source crops in the receiving layout, including expression, camera/body relationship, context and detail where useful. Keep photographic quality separate from polished styling: candid, staged and self-authored images can each be crisp or poorly made.
- **Color:** scope treatment to the intended medium, then place field, image, text and active-state relationships into the composition. A UI palette does not set photographic gamut. Swatches omit occupied area and adjacency. Use [color](color.md) for rendered pair checks.
- **Form/material:** test the recurring edge, cut, curve, light or texture at more than one relevant scale. It should connect parts of the work without making every element identical. Check contact, reflection and texture scale before adding effects.
- **Time:** where change is the idea, sketch its beginning, meaningful transformation and end together. A beautiful resting frame cannot establish the transition.

Change the consequential variable, inspect, then keep or reject the study. Do not interpret a fixed study count or grid as a requirement. When direction is already supported, proceed to construction rather than manufacture alternatives.

## Translate rather than imitate

From a reference, retain a relationship and adapt its cause. An expansive title may need different line breaks in a new language. A close image crop may transfer as a strong detail-to-context contrast, not the same subject coordinates. If a dramatic device conflicts with the task, find another carrier for its visual force rather than quietly deleting the ambition.

Translate across media through perceptual relationships, not copied units: page-turn cadence, screen-state contrast, sightline, silhouette, material joint or musical pause. A system may be consistently rich; it need not reserve all expression for one isolated moment.

## Finish at three scales

View the work as a thumbnail for silhouette and rhythm, at intended size for reading and associations, and at source-detail scale for image/type/material fidelity. Inspect the actual export, not just the editable canvas. Revisit optical margins, cut lines, crop tangencies, spacing transitions, mixed-script weight and shadow consistency.

Keep the decisive study and a short choice note in the private project. They are evidence of exploration, not proof that the final result is good. A successful aesthetic remains a contextual judgment; no numeric taste threshold applies. Ordinary work refines; [first pass](first-pass.md) freezes before whole-output review only when explicitly selected.

For connected pages, compose the sequence relationship before polishing isolated layouts. For scene-led imagery, styling, activity and camera/context must hold together before detail is treated as finish. Explicit shape or naming corrections remain target-scoped, not aesthetic defaults.
