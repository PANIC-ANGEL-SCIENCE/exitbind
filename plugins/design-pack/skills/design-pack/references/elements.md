# Coordinate the relevant elements

The user should not have to enumerate every design decision. Inspect existing identity, choose the elements the medium needs, and resolve them together. Exact values belong to the project; no universal palette, type scale, radius or texture is prescribed.

## Color

Scope constraints to the intended medium, then decide field, image, text, action, selection and status relationships in the actual composition. UI color rules do not automatically grade photography. Compare occupied area, lightness, chroma and local adjacency, not swatches alone. Measure rendered foreground/background pairs and preserve non-color state cues. Physical colors need the producer's specification and proof. Use [color](color.md) for the decision and checking process.

## Type

Set real titles, paragraphs, labels, numbers and translations early. Choose actual faces/weights for role, language, voice and rights; inspect fallback and export. Compare apparent mass and visible baselines rather than nominal size alone. Compose display line endings deliberately while preserving flexible readable UI/body text. Use [typography](typography.md).

## Space and geometry

Allocate space to meaning before boxes. Choose a grid, content-led alignment, typographic field or other structure from the intended reading/exploration. Group related content, create purposeful intervals and inspect edge relationships. Optical alignment may differ from box alignment. Test narrow/wide or near/far contexts rather than resizing a screenshot and calling it adaptation.

## Icons and form

Compare perceived icon weight, baseline and silhouette at use size. Keep stable targets around optically adjusted marks. Match symbols to real actions; labels help when meaning is uncertain. Curves, cuts, borders and silhouettes can carry identity without making every control identical. Test the actual nested edges, crop and focus state through [interface craft](interface-craft.md). Use [iconography](iconography.md) for semantic roles, family geometry, native-size optical study and accessible integration; use [components](components.md) for reusable control contracts.

## Image and material

Choose the image's job, then subject/viewpoint, light, crop and scale together. Authentic content and photographic craft are separate axes. Preserve evidence and use clean reusable sources; exact text/UI should stay editable when needed. Use [Mockup Image](../../mockup-image/SKILL.md).

Light and shadow can express atmosphere as well as hierarchy. Check contact, softness, direction and compositing. Texture scale must fit the medium and output; grain is not an automatic finish. Physical feel, durability and true material identity require evidence beyond a render.

## Motion and sound

Define trigger → meaningful change → stable state → interruption/reversal. Derive timing from distance, frequency and reading, not a fixed luxury-speed table. Preserve reduced-motion and silent equivalents for essential information. Listen and play back the actual output when possible; waveforms and storyboards are not substitutes. Use [motion and sound](motion-sound.md).

## Put the choices together

Use [visual methods](visual-methods.md) for actual-content studies and source-layer production, [aesthetic construction](aesthetic-construction.md) for coupled expression and [brand system](brand-system.md) for real reuse. Test the integrated artifact at thumbnail, intended use and detail. An excellent asset or isolated token cannot prove the whole relationship works.
