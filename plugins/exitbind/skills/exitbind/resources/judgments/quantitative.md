# Quantitative hypothesis work

Restate the engineering decision in ordinary terms. Identify variables,
observables, assumptions, boundaries, state changes and unknowns. Select only
mathematical or physical ideas that fit those facts, and map every useful
concept to a concrete element of the system. State where the correspondence
breaks down.

For each candidate derive a falsifiable prediction and the cheapest useful
measurement or experiment. Discard an analogy that adds no decision-relevant
information; stop when further abstraction would not alter the choice.
Mathematical proof depends on its stated assumptions. An analogy, model or
simulation is not by itself evidence of operational safety, performance or
security. Deliver the smallest useful hypothesis and its residual uncertainty;
the assignment does not grant implementation or acceptance authority.
